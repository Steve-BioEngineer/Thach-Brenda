How did we meet?

“How did we meet?”

People keep asking us that… Well, we met, fell in love instantly, passionately adored each other, and then one day, the doctor said we should get married—and here we are!

It’s true! It was love at first sight—if you count Ms. Lam’s eye rolls and Mr. Pham’s stubborn silences. We had known each other for a while, but most of that time was spent arguing or giving each other the cold shoulder. Roses were red, and honestly… Thach’s face was black.

Then fate stepped in and threw us together on a duo work trip. Somewhere between shared deadlines and unexpected teamwork, we started to understand each other—beyond the sarcasm and stubbornness. That’s when things changed. We didn’t just see each other, we really saw each other.

Then one day, Dr. Pham told Dr. Lam he wanted to marry her. And Dr. Lam agreed.

So here we are—inviting you to celebrate our unexpected, imperfect, and perfect-in-its-own-way love story.
